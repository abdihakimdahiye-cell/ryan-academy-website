About Ryan Academy

Ryan Academy is a private educational institution committed to providing quality education while nurturing strong Islamic values, good character, discipline and academic excellence.

Our History

Ryan Academy was established in January 2005 in Eastleigh, Nairobi, Kenya, with the aim of providing quality education and nurturing responsible learners.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence that develops knowledgeable, confident and responsible learners.

Our Mission

To provide quality academic and Islamic education in a supportive environment that equips learners with knowledge, skills, discipline and strong moral values.
