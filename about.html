About Ryan Academy

Who We Are

Ryan Academy is an Islamic educational institution established in January 2005 and located in Eastleigh, Nairobi, Kenya.

We are committed to providing quality education while nurturing strong Islamic values, good character, discipline and academic excellence.

Our Vision

To become a leading institution producing knowledgeable, responsible and morally upright learners.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in a supportive Islamic environment that develops learners academically, socially and spiritually.
